Mathematical Knowledge Map

This personal project grew out of parsing Bourbaki’s books to build a map of mathematical axioms, definitions and theorems, organised by domain and theory. Explore a concept to discover its connections.

Drag to pan · Scroll to zoom · Click a node to follow its connections
