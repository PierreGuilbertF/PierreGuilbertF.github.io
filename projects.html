Software

LidarView

LidarView brings camera images and 3D lidar point clouds together for visualization and processing, with tools for calibration, sensor fusion and SLAM.

During my time at Kitware, I was one of the main developers of LidarView alongside Bastien Jacquet. Together, we developed the first VeloView lidar API and camera–lidar calibration and sensor fusion algorithms, as well as lidar SLAM and camera SLAM.

This open-source software continues to be maintained and improved by Kitware’s computer vision team.
